# Microsoft AB-100 Exam Guide: Agentic AI Business Solutions Architect

## Introduction

This repository is a study guide for professionals preparing for **Microsoft AB-100: Agentic AI Business Solutions Architect**. It focuses on planning, designing, deploying, and governing AI-powered business solutions using Microsoft technologies.

AB-100 is an advanced, architect-level exam. Candidates should be familiar with Microsoft business applications, AI services, enterprise architecture, security, and solution delivery.

## Exam Overview

- **Exam code:** AB-100
- **Exam title:** Agentic AI Business Solutions Architect
- **Certification:** Microsoft Certified: Agentic AI Business Solutions Architect
- **Level:** Expert
- **Exam language:** English
- **Passing score:** 700 or greater
- **Exam duration and pricing:** Check the current Microsoft exam page.

## Who Should Take It?

This exam is intended for experienced solution architects, technical leaders, and senior consultants who design and deliver AI-powered business solutions.

Relevant experience includes:

- Designing AI-driven enterprise architectures
- Integrating agents and copilots into business applications
- Working with Dynamics 365, Power Platform, and Microsoft AI services
- Designing secure, scalable, and integrated solutions
- Managing AI solution deployment, governance, and lifecycle
- Evaluating business outcomes and return on investment (ROI)

## Study Notes by Domain

### 1. Plan AI-Powered Business Solutions

Review how to:

- Analyze business requirements and determine where agents can automate tasks, support analytics, and improve decision-making.
- Evaluate data quality, relevance, timeliness, availability, and suitability for grounding.
- Develop an AI adoption strategy and roadmap.
- Design multi-agent solutions using Microsoft 365 Copilot, Copilot Studio, and Microsoft Foundry.
- Decide when to use prebuilt agents, custom agents, custom models, or extensions.
- Establish prompt engineering guidelines and prompt libraries.
- Evaluate build-versus-buy decisions.
- Estimate total cost of ownership and analyze ROI.

### 2. Design AI-Powered Business Solutions

Study:

- Copilot and agent integration with Dynamics 365 applications.
- Task agents, autonomous agents, prompt-and-response agents, and agent flows.
- Copilot Studio topics, fallback behavior, actions, and knowledge sources.
- Microsoft Foundry models, tools, and custom AI solutions.
- Grounding, data processing, and model selection.
- Microsoft 365 Copilot extensibility.
- Model Context Protocol (MCP) and Agent2Agent (A2A).
- Power Platform architecture and the Well-Architected Framework.
- Integration of AI features across business applications.

### 3. Deploy AI-Powered Business Solutions

Review:

- Agent monitoring, telemetry, feedback, and performance tuning.
- Testing strategies, validation criteria, and end-to-end test scenarios.
- Application lifecycle management (ALM) for agents, models, connectors, and data.
- Environment strategy and deployment governance.
- Responsible AI principles and risk management.
- Model and agent security.
- Prompt manipulation risks and mitigation strategies.
- Data residency, access controls, audit trails, and compliance.
- Continuous improvement based on usage and performance metrics.

These topic summaries are study aids. Use Microsoft's latest skills-measured document for the complete objective list.

## Practical Lab Ideas

Use authorized Microsoft environments and follow organizational policies.

1. Map a business process and identify tasks suitable for agent assistance.
2. Design a solution diagram showing applications, agents, data sources, and integrations.
3. Prototype a Copilot Studio agent using approved knowledge sources.
4. Compare a prebuilt agent with a custom-agent approach for a sample requirement.
5. Create a test plan covering expected behavior, failures, and security boundaries.
6. Draft an ALM process for moving an agent between development and production environments.
7. Define monitoring metrics and a feedback process.
8. Create a simple ROI analysis using clearly stated assumptions.
9. Review data access and governance risks in a hypothetical enterprise solution.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

- Preparing from an outdated exam outline.
- Focusing on individual tools without understanding the overall architecture.
- Choosing an agent approach without considering requirements and constraints.
- Ignoring data quality, permissions, and grounding.
- Neglecting testing, monitoring, ALM, or governance.
- Overlooking costs, ROI, and operational responsibilities.
- Treating practice questions as a substitute for real understanding.
- Using unauthorized exam dumps or leaked questions.
